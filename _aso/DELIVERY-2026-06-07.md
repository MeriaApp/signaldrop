- **Option B — stay paid $4.99.** Lean on the new ratings prompt + earned reviews. Lower effort, slower, leaves the velocity/ratings problem mostly unsolved.

**Decision needed:** A or B. The ratings prompt + metadata + screenshot reorder ship regardless; Option A additionally requires the freemium build (separate work, only on your go — it touches revenue and existing buyers).
